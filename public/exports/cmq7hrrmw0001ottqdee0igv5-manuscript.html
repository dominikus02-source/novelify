Chapter 1: Chapter 1: The Beginning

Original

Di sebuah desa terpencil di kaki gunung, hiduplah seorang gadis bernama Aria. Ia memiliki mata yang bersinar seperti embun pagi, dan rambut hitam panjang yang selalu diterbangkan angin. Desa itu dikenal dengan nama Kampung Awan, karena selalu diselimuti kabut tipis yang menambah kesan mistis.

Translation

In a remote village at the foot of a mountain, there lived a girl named Aria. She had eyes that shone like morning dew and long black hair that was always tousled by the wind. The village was known as Kampung Awan, or the Village of Clouds, for it was perpetually shrouded in a thin mist that added to its mystical aura.
